| D-012 | Open | Decide the account/auth provider and recovery model. | The current password gate is not user authentication; provider choice affects sessions, email, deletion, and tests. | At the start of [#22](https://github.com/marcossnikel/training-hub/issues/22), after Platform & Quality supplies options and trade-offs. |
| D-013 | Accepted | Request exactly `activity:read_all,profile:read_all` and enforce the granted scope before a connection becomes usable. Encrypted connection material and imported/derived Strava data exist only while authorization is active. A delete request, Strava revocation/account deletion, or revoking disconnect permanently deletes that material promptly, no later than 30 days. A retained-history/pause-sync model is not accepted. | These are privacy and onboarding promises, not merely implementation details. | A written Strava policy clarification requires a reviewed change. |
| D-014 | Open | Confirm whether the first beta has a manual invite/waitlist gate before self-serve sign-up. | A small invited cohort may be safer while BYO setup and support are still being learned. | Before landing page and public beta release. |
| D-015 | Accepted | The first comparable-session insight is a deterministic **comparable prior activity**: one athlete's confirmed current activity matched to one of that same athlete's confirmed prior activities using only sport family, distance, and moving time. A reliable match requires distance difference less than or equal to 10% and moving-time difference less than or equal to 20%, each relative to the current activity. There is no limited-confidence tier. | Current summaries do not contain workout intent or a general training-block model. This gives an athlete a transparent, evidence-linked comparison without inventing a workout classification, equivalence, or coaching conclusion. | A separately accepted decision adds trustworthy workout classification, a general block model, or a new similarity input. |
| D-016 | Accepted | Each athlete registers the exact callback URL for the current environment with the Strava app they control. In deployed environments, Training Hub derives that URL only from the non-secret server-configured canonical HTTPS `TRAINING_HUB_PUBLIC_ORIGIN`; local development may derive only a direct loopback (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, or `[::1]`) request origin and ignores forwarded headers. It never accepts a browser-supplied callback or redirect URL. | The BYO beta path must not depend on a shared/founder app, a pre-provisioned public callback, or spoofable proxy headers. An athlete still configures their own app for every environment they use. | When a reviewed hosted Strava integration changes the connection model. |
| D-017 | Accepted | Disconnect attempts Strava deauthorization, then immediately and permanently deletes the current athlete’s local connection material and Strava-origin graph even when provider revocation cannot be confirmed. It preserves manually entered activities, goals, journals, thresholds, manual gear, the account, and unrelated data. A completed first sync lands in the recent training log. | Local privacy control must not be held hostage by an external provider response, while the first useful moment is verification of imported records rather than an unsupported inference. | A reviewed provider lifecycle or data-origin model changes the deletion inventory. |

## Decision protocol

Product & Architecture owns the intent and acceptance criteria. Platform & Quality owns feasibility, security, environments, and reproducible verification. A feature builder may propose a change, but does not silently overturn an Accepted decision: record the trade-off here and obtain a new decision before implementation proceeds.
