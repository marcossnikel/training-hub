**Started:** 2026-08-27
**Target consolidation deadline:** approximately 2026-08-28 02:15 BRT
**Product boundary:** an English-language premium mobile Performance Practice

**Release boundary:** complete v1, not a reduced MVP

This artifact sharpens the provisional thesis. It does not authorize code,

decisions.

## Executive verdict

Keep the Performance Practice System thesis, with five hard corrections.

1. The product is private-first in value and community-complete in release.
   The private loop must earn continued use when the community has no people or
   posts. The social system still ships as a first-class part of complete v1.
2. A Practice Pack is a versioned governance and field contract, not a reusable
   protocol form. Multi-domain support is coherent only when the stable layer is
   the learning loop and each domain owns its fields, eligibility, safety, and
   publication rules.
3. Public object type, evidence label, person verification, moderation state,
   and Pack support level are independent dimensions. No badge, author role,
   follower relationship, moderator action, or branded item may silently
   upgrade evidence.
4. The social network exists to improve interpretation and start a better
   private Experiment. It does not exist to maximize posting, reactions, time
   in feed, or creator status.
5. The largest existential risk is operational, not conceptual. A complete
   social v1 requires credible moderation, credential review, commercial
   conflict handling, privacy operations, and correction paths before release.

The category sentence is:

> A Performance Practice System helps endurance athletes turn performance
> ideas into bounded practice inside the training plan they already trust,
> record planned versus actual execution, and form honest personal Findings
> with source-labelled community context.

The category is defensible as a product-quality thesis. None of its individual
features is unique. The distinctive mechanism is the complete chain from a
domain-governed Practice Pack to a private Finding and, when chosen, from a
structured public object back into a rebuilt private Experiment.

## Primary athlete and market position

The primary user is not a novice looking for a training plan. The primary user
is a committed amateur or competitive endurance athlete who already has a plan,
coach, practitioner, or self-selected protocol and repeatedly asks:

> I tried this in training. What exactly did I plan, what actually happened,
> what was different, and what is worth doing next?

| Participant | Product relationship | What they need | What the product must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Athlete with a coach or practitioner | Primary user | Preserve instruction, execute deliberately, record context, make the next bounded decision | The app has become another coach |
| Experienced self-directed athlete | Primary user | State their own protocol authority, avoid hindsight reconstruction, compare similar-enough Tries | Self-direction makes the setup scientifically controlled |
| Coach | Contributor and authority for a chosen athlete | Literal role state, scoped protocol context, source-labelled discussion | Coaching practice is research evidence |
| Sports dietitian | Contributor and authority for a chosen athlete | Credential scope, protocol provenance, safety boundaries | A credential makes every claim correct |
| Researcher | Contributor and research-context source | Accurate citations, population and limitation fields, corrections | ORCID, affiliation, or title upgrades a paper |
| Commercial representative | Constrained participant | Literal affiliation, factual product answers, visible conflicts | A brand relationship can be hidden in an athlete story |

### Primary conflict

The athlete wants to learn from repeated practice, but existing notes lose
structure, performance apps often prescribe or score, generic experiment tools
overstate control, and social feeds reward confidence and popularity. The
product wins only if it makes disciplined reflection easier than Notes while
making uncertainty more visible than a feed or dashboard.

### Market awareness and sophistication

| Stage | Athlete state | Product response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem aware | Knows race execution or another practice feels inconsistent, but reconstructs it from memory | Show the cost of losing planned-versus-actual context |
| Solution aware | Uses Notes, spreadsheets, fueling calculators, sensors, or training platforms | Show why planning, tracking, and a bounded Finding belong in one loop |
| Product aware | Compares this product with trackers, self-experiment apps, and athlete networks | Demonstrate Pack-specific authority and safety plus structured social provenance |
| Most aware | Has completed a real Experiment and knows the next decision changed | Make the second Experiment faster and more useful, not more elaborate |

The primary sophistication target is level 4: an athlete already exposed to
personalization, tracking, dashboards, and confident performance claims. A
generic promise to optimize performance will not land. The mechanism must show
how the app protects the athlete from hindsight, context loss, false certainty,
and social-status cues.

## Autonomous grilling decision tree

The rounds below follow the grilling method, but resolve decisions
autonomously because this task explicitly requests adversarial refinement
without waiting for user answers.

### Round 1: product thesis

**Q1. Does the product solve a recurring job or merely provide a careful way to
document niche tests?**

Recommended answer: the recurring job is performance-practice memory and
decision quality. The athlete should repeatedly need the loop before important
sessions and after meaningful Tries. If Findings do not change Keep, Repeat,
Change one thing, Stop, or Not enough evidence decisions, the thesis fails.

**Q2. Is Performance Practice System a real category or internal language?**

Recommended answer: use it as a provisional category descriptor, not a claim
that the market already recognizes it. Lead with the concrete job and mechanism.
Test whether athletes understand practice as rehearsal plus reflection, rather
than generic training.

**Q3. Does social make the product stronger, or only more expensive and risky?**

Recommended answer: social earns its place only through three outcomes:
source-aware interpretation, continuity through followed people and Packs, and
linked private Experiments. Generic consumption or posting is not success.

**Resolution:** retain the thesis. Treat changed private decisions and
public-to-private handoffs as the two value proofs.

### Round 2: Practice Packs and multi-domain coherence

**Q4. What is genuinely shared across Fueling, Heat, and Supplementation?**

Recommended answer: the five-object private loop, planned-versus-actual
structure, context preservation, authority provenance, evidence labelling,
revision, privacy, and next-decision vocabulary.

**Q5. What must never be generalized?**

Recommended answer: exposure fields, eligibility, stop rules, calculations,
comparison rules, public language, protocol ownership, and publication review.

**Q6. Is Performance Supplementation one coherent Pack?**

Recommended answer: no. It is a Practice family. Sodium bicarbonate and
structured observation of an emerging Tested Item need distinct Pack contracts
or Pack modules with different support levels. A family organizes discovery and
moderation. It does not authorize one shared supplement protocol.

**Resolution:** a new domain joins only when it passes a Pack admission test.
The shared engine must not require a universal score, universal outcome, or
universal protocol.

### Round 3: social objects

**Q7. Why not allow a flexible post composer and label the result afterward?**

Recommended answer: because free-form composition lets advice, dosage,
commercial endorsement, and causal language appear before the product knows
which contract applies. The author chooses an object type first and completes
its required structure.

**Q8. Are post type and evidence label the same thing?**

Recommended answer: no. A Research Note is a public object type. Statements
inside it may be Research evidence, Emerging research, Commercial claim, or
Open question. A Finding may contain Athlete observation, App comparison, and
Athlete interpretation. Each substantive statement keeps its label.

**Q9. Should active Experiment Updates exist?**

Recommended answer: yes, because complete v1 explicitly includes them. They
share question, authority, progress, and selected context. They may not share
an interim verdict, raw Field Note, copied dose, or implied trend.

**Resolution:** preserve all five accepted post types. Use schema-first
composition and claim-level labels.

### Round 4: follows, feeds, and discovery

**Q10. What can a user follow?**

Recommended answer: people and published Practice Packs. Use Pack as the
canonical followable domain object. Unsupported topics may host eligible Open
Questions, but they are browsed or saved, not followed as if they had a Pack
contract.

**Q11. Should follower and following counts or lists ever be public in v1?**

Recommended answer: no. Hide exact counts and lists from third parties.
Owners retain a private Connections screen for followers, following, removals,
mutes, blocks, and follow requests.

**Q12. How does discovery work without popularity?**

Recommended answer: explicit Pack compatibility, selected interests, object
type, language, recency, source completeness, and literal role filters. Every
non-chronological discovery result explains why it appears. Follower count,
reaction count, commercial spend, and discussion volume are excluded.

**Resolution:** Following and Packs feeds are reverse chronological. Discovery
is a separate transparent browse mode inside Feed and Library.

### Round 5: roles and credentials

**Q13. What does verification prove?**

Recommended answer: only the literal check displayed. Identity checked means a
named identity was checked by a stated method on a date. Credential checked
means an issuer, credential, jurisdiction, and expiry or recheck date were
checked. Affiliation checked means an organization confirmed an affiliation on
a date.

**Q14. Can credentials gate publication?**

Recommended answer: they may gate a public object whose type asserts qualified
practice, such as a Practitioner Protocol Note. This is an eligibility and
safety rule, not an evidence upgrade. Research Notes remain judged by their
sources, not the author's badge.

**Q15. Is ORCID sufficient researcher verification?**

Recommended answer: no. An authenticated ORCID connection establishes control
of an ORCID record, not verified identity. Pair it with an independent identity
or affiliation check and display each result literally.

**Resolution:** role, identity, credential, and affiliation are separate
facets. None changes evidence status or bypasses moderation.

### Round 6: commercial pressure and branded Tested Items

**Q16. Can NØMIO have a page without turning the product into an ad surface?**

Recommended answer: yes, if the page is a neutral Tested Item record that
separates manufacturer claims, independent research, batch or formulation
identity, athlete Findings, conflicts, corrections, and Open Questions.

**Q17. Are affiliate links compatible with the evidence model?**

Recommended answer: no for v1. Affiliate links, discount codes, sponsored
placement, paid ranking, and pay-to-boost discovery create avoidable pressure
on object eligibility and interpretation.

**Q18. Can a commercial representative contribute?**

Recommended answer: yes, under a literal commercial role and mandatory
post-level affiliation disclosure. They may answer factual product or batch
questions, add primary sources, and raise Open Questions. Promotional calls to
action and undisclosed endorsements are prohibited. A representative, employee,
owner, client, affiliate, or paid endorser may not publish a Finding about the
affiliated product in v1. Disclosure exposes the conflict, but it does not
remove the transaction incentive or make the object independent.

**Resolution:** branded items are legitimate context objects, never Practice
families or evidence grades. V1 has no affiliate economy.

### Round 7: moderation and misinformation

**Q19. Can labels replace moderation?**

Recommended answer: no. Labels help comprehension, but dangerous protocols,
misleading claims, harassment, hidden advertising, credential fraud, and
privacy leaks still need prevention, reports, human judgment, and appeals.

**Q20. Should moderators decide scientific truth?**

Recommended answer: only within bounded policy. Moderators verify required
sources, labels, conflicts, prohibited language, Pack eligibility, and safety
rules. They may mark a claim disputed or unsupported. They do not silently
rewrite interpretation or claim that peer review makes a conclusion true.

**Q21. Can a small team support a global social network?**

Recommended answer: not safely by assumption. Complete v1 needs a cohort,
coverage window, queue caps, temporary hiding rules, escalation, one primary
and one backup moderator, and a demonstrated workload before community release.

**Resolution:** moderation capacity is a release gate. An empty or paused public
queue must never disable the private product.

### Round 8: cold start and private usefulness

**Q22. What appears when there are no real contributors?**

Recommended answer: reviewed app-owned Guides, followed Pack updates, clearly
editorial Open Questions, the athlete's saved objects, and the next private
Practice action. Empty people and Finding directories remain honestly empty.

**Q23. May the founder seed the network?**

Recommended answer: only with real identity, real disclosures, and authentic
contributions. No synthetic accounts, fabricated Findings, staged discussions,
or inflated counts.

**Q24. What if an athlete never opens Feed?**

Recommended answer: onboarding, Experiment planning, Try, Field Note,
comparison, Finding, reminders, export, and deletion must remain complete.

**Resolution:** onboarding follows Packs before it follows people. Private
utility does not depend on public inventory.

### Round 9: safety, authority, and data integrity

**Q25. Is the athlete's coach always authoritative?**

Recommended answer: the chosen coach or practitioner is authoritative for the
athlete's instruction inside an existing plan. That authority never overrides
stop rules, prohibited public content, Pack eligibility, evidence labels,
moderation, privacy, or data integrity.

**Q26. What happens when an external instruction conflicts with a Pack?**

Recommended answer: the athlete may record it privately as External
instruction when legal and safe to record. The app does not approve it, turn it
into a template, calculate from it beyond allowed descriptive fields, or copy
it to another athlete.

**Q27. How does deletion preserve both privacy and public integrity?**

Recommended answer: public objects unpublish immediately, private data enters
the disclosed deletion path, and necessary thread structure uses content-free
tombstones. The product preserves correction and moderation audit facts only to
the minimum extent required and discloses retention. It does not keep a public
athlete story merely because replies depend on it.

**Resolution:** authority is scoped, safety is non-delegable, and a public
object is a minimized revision rather than a visibility flag on private data.

### Round 10: complete-v1 coherence

**Q28. Is complete v1 too large to validate?**

Recommended answer: it is large, especially because social operations are part
of the product. The response is staged proof with stop gates, not a reduced
release. Failure of a gate stops or changes the thesis before launch.

**Q29. Can an invitation cohort count as complete release?**

Recommended answer: yes if every accepted surface is functional and available
to the cohort. Cohort size is a rollout boundary. Missing Feed, roles,
moderation, deletion, or a Pack is a product-scope reduction and is not allowed
under this contract.

**Q30. What should be killed independently?**

Recommended answer: private usefulness and the community have separate kill
decisions. A useful private tool does not justify an unsafe community. A lively
community does not justify a private loop that fails to change decisions.

**Resolution:** keep complete v1. Make release conditional on both private and
social proof.

## Canonical domain model

The central model is:

Practice Pack -> Experiment -> Try -> Field Note -> Finding

The public handoff is:

minimized revisioned public object -> bounded discussion -> rebuilt private
Experiment

| Term | Canonical meaning | Explicitly not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice family | Discovery and moderation grouping such as Fueling, Heat, or Performance Supplementation | A protocol or a shared scientific method |
| Practice Pack | Reviewed, versioned contract for fields, authority, comparisons, safety, evidence language, publication, and moderation | A content tag or creator template |
| Practice Guide | App-owned source-backed explanation of a Pack | Personalized instruction |
| Protocol | Versioned plan the athlete intends to follow, with visible authority | A copied social post |
| Tested Item | Named product, food, supplement, or equipment used inside a Practice | A Practice family, recommendation, or evidence grade |
| Experiment | Private bounded question, intended decision variable, assumptions, horizon, and observations | A causal trial claim |
| Try | One planned opportunity to execute a Protocol | A result |
| Field Note | Private planned-versus-actual record of one Try | A public post |
| Finding | Revisable personal synthesis across eligible Field Notes | Proof, advice, or universal result |
| Public object | Separate minimized revision derived from eligible material or a structured source contribution | A public flag on a private record |
| Linked Experiment | New private Experiment rebuilt from an eligible public object with attribution | Replication or one-tap protocol copy |

### Domain invariants

1. Every Experiment belongs to exactly one Practice Pack version.
2. A Practice family never supplies execution rules.
3. A Try records the protocol version used at planning time.
4. Planned and actual values remain separate.
5. Missing, stopped, deviated, and incomparable Tries remain visible.
6. A Finding cites the Field Notes and Pack version it synthesizes.
7. Publishing creates a separate minimized revision.
8. A public correction creates a new revision and preserves a visible
   correction relationship.
9. A linked Experiment rebuilds eligibility, targets, authority, and safety.
10. No role, credential, popularity cue, moderation action, or brand changes
    evidence status.

## Five independent public dimensions

This separation is the most important refinement.

| Dimension | Question answered | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public object type | What structure is being published? | Finding, Experiment Update, Research Note, Practitioner Protocol Note, Open Question |
| Evidence label | What kind of statement is this? | Research evidence, Coach practice, Athlete observation, Commercial claim |
| Person state | What was literally checked about the author? | Self-described, Identity checked, Credential checked, Affiliation checked |
| Moderation state | Where is the object in the workflow? | Draft, eligibility failed, pending review, published, corrected, hidden, withdrawn |
| Pack support level | What may the product guide or copy? | Guided Practice, External-Protocol Companion, Structured observation |

A verified researcher may publish a weak preprint. An unverified athlete may
cite a strong consensus statement. A moderator may publish an eligible object
without endorsing its interpretation. A popular Finding remains an athlete
interpretation. A branded Tested Item remains commercial context.

## Evidence and claim taxonomy

Consensus and position statements are a source subtype within Research
evidence, not a status badge above the taxonomy.

| Label | Meaning | Allowed use | Never means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research evidence | A cited peer-reviewed study, systematic review, consensus, or position statement, with source subtype, population, result, and limits | Practice Guide context and Research Notes | Automatic personalization or universal truth |
| Emerging research | A preprint, early result, or immature body of evidence | Research Note or Open Question with prominent maturity state | Product promise or stable recommendation |
| Coach or practitioner instruction | The selected person's instruction for this athlete and Practice | Private Protocol authority, subject to safety | Research consensus or public template |
| Coach practice | A practitioner's reported method, population, and scope | Practitioner Protocol Note and discussion | Established evidence |
| Commercial claim | A claim from a manufacturer, seller, affiliate, employee, or sponsored party | Tested Item context with disclosure | Independent evidence |
| Athlete observation | What the athlete recorded during one or more Tries | Field Note, Experiment Update, and Finding inputs | Causation or advice |
| App comparison | A deterministic descriptive comparison of entered data | Differences, missingness, and limitations | Adaptation, efficacy, or causation |
| Athlete interpretation | The athlete's bounded meaning and next decision | Finding | Proof for another athlete |
| Open question | A precise unresolved question with known and unknown context | Discussion and research direction | A disguised recommendation |

One statement gets one primary label. A public object may contain several
labelled statement blocks. Quoted commercial claims retain Commercial claim
even when posted by a researcher. An athlete's summary of a paper is separated
into Research evidence for the cited result and Athlete interpretation for
their conclusion.

### Product relationship is separate from evidence

Every branded public object and relevant reply also carries one product
relationship state:

- bought at ordinary retail;
- free product;
- discount;
- payment;
- affiliate or referral benefit;
- employee, owner, or client;
- family or personal relationship;
- research funding;
- other benefit; or
- no known relationship.

The relationship is an author attestation with a timestamp. It appears before
substantive text on the full object and on the compact feed card. It survives
revisions, direct links, app-generated share pointers, quoted replies, and item
pages. A profile disclosure may repeat it but never replaces the object-local
disclosure.

An athlete observation remains an Athlete observation after an unsolicited
gift, with prominent Free product metadata and higher-risk review. Brand-run
gifted-to-post campaigns and compensation contingent on posting or sentiment
are not publicly eligible in v1. The product does not reuse a community Finding
in first-party marketing, item-page hero content, or an App Store screenshot.

## Practice Pack admission and v1 contracts

### Pack admission test

A domain becomes guided or publicly executable only when all answers below are
yes.

| Admission question | Required proof |
| --- | --- |
| Is the bounded user question explicit? | The Pack names supported and unsupported questions |
| Can planned and actual execution be represented honestly? | Domain fields preserve both without a universal score |
| Is protocol authority explicit? | Every allowed authority state and its scope are defined |
| Are observable outcomes descriptive? | No proxy is presented as adaptation, efficacy, or diagnosis |
| Are confounders and missing context visible? | The schema preserves differences instead of normalizing them away |
| Are stop and escalation rules reviewed? | Safety owner, wording, and prohibited flows are named |
| Are comparison rules bounded? | The Pack defines when Tries are similar enough and when they are not |
| Is public language constrained? | Allowed post types, prohibited claims, and copied fields are explicit |
| Is moderation operable? | Queue, reviewer skill, escalation, and pause controls exist |
| Are sources reviewable? | Primary sources, review date, conflicts, and update owner are present |

If one answer is no, the subject remains a browsable topic or private generic
Experiment. It does not receive a guided Protocol.

### Race Fueling

**Support level:** Guided Practice.

The Pack may structure an athlete-, coach-, or dietitian-owned intake target,
product plan, timing, carrying plan, adherence, conditions, gut comfort, energy
steadiness, practicality, perceived effort, deviations, and the next rehearsal
decision.

It may not prescribe hydration or sodium, diagnose gastrointestinal symptoms,
optimize body weight, recommend a commercial product, or infer intake from a
wearable. The current evidence boundary remains grounded in the
[World Athletics nutrition consensus](https://worldathletics.org/download/download?filename=23fb9de0-6699-4d5b-b075-42f5da5518f5.pdf&urlslug=Nutrition+for+Athletics+-+2019+IAAF+Consensus+Statement).

### Heat Practice

**Support level:** External-Protocol Companion.

The athlete must already have an eligible externally owned protocol. The Pack
records the owner, version, exposure method, environment, existing-session
workload, planned versus actual exposure, perceived strain, interruptions,
recovery, warning signs, and missing context.

The app does not generate temperature, duration, dehydration, clothing,
core-temperature, or return-to-practice targets. It does not infer body
temperature, certify safety, diagnose heat illness, or declare acclimation. A
warning sign or unsafe instruction stops the flow and routes the athlete outside
the app. Relevant primary sources include the
[IOC heat consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9811094/) and
[NATA exertional heat illness position statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4639891/).

### Performance Supplementation

**Support level:** a family containing distinct Pack contracts.

Performance Supplementation must not become one generic dose-and-result form.
Complete v1 includes:

- sodium bicarbonate as an External-Protocol Companion;
- structured observation for athlete-selected branded or unbranded Tested
  Items such as NØMIO;
- an optional private supplementation baseline in onboarding; and
- supplement, adverse-effect, commercial-conflict, and anti-doping boundaries.

The sodium bicarbonate path records an externally received protocol,
formulation, amount and timing as received, adherence, gastrointestinal or
other adverse effects, and context. It never generates or copies a dose. The
[ISSN position stand](https://jissn.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12970-021-00458-w)
and [AIS Group A framework](https://www.ais.gov.au/nutrition/supplements/group_a)
support an individualized and scenario-specific boundary.

### NØMIO and branded Tested Items

NØMIO remains a Tested Item, not a Pack, family, mechanism, or evidence label.
A neutral item page contains:

1. exact brand, product, formulation, and batch when known;
2. manufacturer statements labelled Commercial claim;
3. primary independent sources with population and conflict fields;
4. corrections, retractions, and formulation changes;
5. eligible personal Findings;
6. mandatory commercial relationships; and
7. Open Questions.

The product page may link to the official
[NØMIO science page](https://drinknomio.com/en-no/pages/science), while keeping
the small related study, preprint, other formulations, conflicts, and
anti-doping limits separate. A Cologne List entry can be recorded as a batch
testing fact. It may not be presented as proof of efficacy, universal purity,
or a recommendation.

No affiliate link, discount code, sponsored placement, paid boost, brand-funded
ranking, or pay-to-review path exists in v1.

## Complete v1 experience contract

### Navigation

The bottom navigation is:

Practice | Feed | Library | Profile

Each surface owns a clear job.

| Surface | Primary job | Required empty state | Required failure state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Practice | Continue the next private Practice action | Create the first real Experiment from a followed Pack | Preserve drafts, explain retry, never lose a Field Note |
| Feed | Follow real people and Pack activity, discover structured objects | Show honest Pack editorial content and empty people contributions | Preserve chronology and show unavailable or withdrawn objects accurately |
| Library | Find Guides, Pack versions, saved objects, followed discussions, and own publications | Recommend reviewed Packs without fabricating community activity | Show stale, corrected, retracted, or unavailable source state |
| Profile | Manage identity, roles, connections, privacy, export, and deletion | Private athlete profile with no public contribution pressure | Preserve account control even if credential or moderation services are down |

### Login and signup

- Passwordless email-code login and signup.
- Clear recovery and expired-code behavior.
- No social account is required.
- Account creation leads into progressive onboarding, not a generic dashboard.
- Account deletion is initiated inside the app.

### Progressive onboarding

Onboarding uses four named chapters:

Baseline -> Focus -> First Try -> Ready

**Baseline** captures sports, training context, existing plan authority, chosen
coach or practitioner, and current practice habits with I do not track this and
I am not sure options.

**Focus** selects the first Practice Pack and bounded question.

**First Try** assembles the actual Experiment and planned Try. It never silently
activates a Protocol.

**Ready** confirms authority, safety boundaries, privacy, reminders, and the
next action.

The optional supplementation baseline includes caffeine, creatine,
electrolytes, vitamins or minerals, bicarbonate, NØMIO or other, none, and
prefer not to answer. It is private, optional, non-medical, and never blocks
Ready.

Progress shows completed chapters and confirmed fields. It does not use a fake
percentage, readiness score, adaptation animation, or personalization claim.

### Private Practice

Complete v1 supports:

- reviewed Practice Guides and versioned Pack contracts;
- Experiment drafts and activation;
- Try planning attached to an existing session;
- planned-versus-actual domain fields;
- context, deviation, missingness, and confounder capture;
- offline Field Note drafts and safe retry;
- similar-enough comparison without matched, controlled, or equivalent claims;
- Keep, Repeat, Change one thing, Stop, Mixed, and Not enough evidence
  Findings;
- manual entry as the source of truth;
- reminders that support the Practice instead of driving engagement;
- history, revision, export, scoped deletion, and account deletion; and
- private generic Experiments for unsupported topics with no public or causal
  output.

The home state prioritizes the next useful action. It does not show a universal
performance score or dense biometric dashboard.

## First-class social system

### Graph

Users follow two canonical object types:

- **Person**, for continuity with an athlete, coach, dietitian, researcher, or
  other real contributor.
- **Practice Pack**, for Guides, Pack revisions, safety notices, Open Questions,
  and eligible public contributions in that Pack.

Public profiles show a Follow control and the current viewer's relationship.
They do not show follower counts, following counts, follower lists, or following
lists. The owner manages all relationships in a private Connections screen.

Mute, block, remove follower, and report are complete-v1 requirements. There
are no public follow achievements, suggested famous accounts, or verified-only
leaderboards.

### Feed

Feed contains three explicit modes.

| Mode | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Following | Approved public objects from deliberately followed people, reverse chronological, filterable by Pack and object type |
| Packs | Official Pack updates and eligible public objects in deliberately followed Packs, reverse chronological |
| Discover | Transparent browse by Pack compatibility, selected interest, object type, language, recency, source completeness, and literal role state |

Operational Pack safety or correction notices may appear above chronology with
an explicit reason. This is a safety interrupt, not engagement ranking.

Discover explains why every result appears. It never uses follower count,
reaction count, discussion volume, save count, commercial payment, or predicted
engagement.

### Public object contracts

| Object | Who may author | Required structure | Publication boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finding | Athlete who owns the completed private Experiment | Pack and Protocol versions, completed Tries, selected context, observations, app comparisons, interpretation, limits, revision | Personal and revisable, no advice or copied target |
| Experiment Update | Athlete who owns the active Experiment | Question, authority, progress, selected context, deviations worth noting | No raw Field Note, interim verdict, dose, or implied trend |
| Research Note | Any eligible identified author | DOI or primary source, source subtype, population, result, limitations, conflicts, correction state | Author role does not upgrade the source |
| Practitioner Protocol Note | Eligible practitioner with required literal checks | Role, credential scope, population, reported practice, authority, sources, contraindication boundary | Coach practice, not one-tap executable instruction |
| Open Question | Any eligible author | Pack or topic, precise question, known context, uncertainty, relevant sources | No disguised recommendation or commercial lead |

Research Notes remain open to athletes because evidence lives in the source.
Practitioner Protocol Notes require the Pack's role and credential eligibility
because the object type asserts qualified practice. That gate does not elevate
Coach practice to Research evidence.

### Discussion

A reply begins with one public intent:

- Ask about context;
- Add a source;
- Share a related observation;
- Offer practitioner perspective;
- Challenge the interpretation; or
- Raise a safety concern.

Raise a safety concern is a visible discussion contribution. Report is a
separate private moderation action. The interface must not confuse them.

Threads have exactly two structural levels. Replies to a second-level reply
remain at level two, ordered chronologically, and identify the addressed author.
This preserves conversation without deep nesting or status-driven pile-ons.

Discussion interactions are Reply, Follow discussion, Save, Start linked
Experiment when eligible, and Report. A visible reply count may support
navigation. There are no likes, kudos, upvotes, public saves, reposts, quote
posts, generic status posts, workout photos, direct messages, trending lists,
or creator scores in v1.

### Linked private Experiment

Start linked Experiment is the social system's highest-value action.

The handoff preserves:

- source object and revision;
- author attribution;
- Practice Pack and version;
- the source question;
- disclosed context and limitations; and
- a visible branch relationship.

The handoff does not copy:

- another athlete's target or dose;
- eligibility;
- practitioner authority;
- product amount or timing;
- heat exposure;
- safety attestations; or
- a conclusion.

The receiving athlete rebuilds the question, intended decision variable,
Protocol authority, Pack eligibility, observations, and first Try. The product
uses linked, not replicated.

- chosen public name and image;
- sports and relevant Packs;

## Roles, identity, affiliation, and credentials

Every account is an athlete account first. Optional public facets are Coach,
Sports dietitian, Researcher, Other practitioner, and Commercial
representative. One person may hold more than one facet, but every relevant
commercial relationship appears on every relevant object.

### Literal states

| Facet | Public wording | Required record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Self-described Coach | Claimed role and date |
| Identity | Identity checked on date | Method class, reviewer or provider, date, expiry if any |
| Credential | Credential checked with issuer on date | Exact credential, issuer, jurisdiction, status, expiry or recheck |
| Affiliation | Affiliation checked with organization on date | Organization, role if confirmed, method, date, expiry or recheck |
| ORCID | ORCID connected | Authenticated ORCID identifier and connection date |
| Lapsed state | Expired or could not be rechecked | Previous state, last check, reason category |

Never use trusted, expert-approved, science verified, top coach, elite
researcher, or expertise scores.

### Researcher path

An authenticated ORCID connection plus an independent identity or affiliation
check supports a literal researcher state. ORCID itself says its identifier is
not an identity-verification system
([ORCID support](https://support.orcid.org/hc/en-us/articles/360006972413-Does-an-ORCID-iD-assure-my-identity)).

Research source metadata may use Crossref and correction or retraction
references, but metadata presence does not validate a conclusion
([Crossref REST API](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/),
[Crossref Retraction Watch data](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/retraction-watch/)).

The verification workflow follows the useful separation in
[NIST SP 800-63A-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63a/proofing/):
resolve an identity, validate evidence or attributes against credible sources,
and verify that the applicant is the rightful subject. This does not make the
product a standards-certified identity provider. It prevents one ambiguous
Verified badge from hiding several different claims.

Evidence status remains source-based. The
[Cochrane Handbook evidence-certainty framework](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14)
considers study methods and limitations such as bias, inconsistency,
indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias. Author popularity, platform
verification, and professional title are not evidence-certainty domains.

### Coach and dietitian paths

Each supported credential requires:

- a named issuer;
- a jurisdiction;
- a current public or issuer-confirmed check method;
- exact credential scope;
- status and expiry;
- recheck cadence;
- appeal and correction path; and
- human review.

English-language distribution does not imply global credential support.
Complete v1 must support at least the declared initial jurisdiction and display
other roles as self-described when no valid verification path exists. The
initial jurisdiction remains unresolved until the launch market and moderator
operation are selected.

### Commercial role

A Commercial representative has a persistent profile disclosure and a
post-level disclosure whenever a brand, employer, client, free product,
sponsorship, affiliate relationship, family relationship, or other material
connection is relevant.

The [FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)
states that material connections should be obvious and placed with the
endorsement itself. V1 therefore does not treat a profile-only disclosure as
sufficient. Other launch jurisdictions require separate review.

Commercial representatives may answer factual formulation or batch questions,
add a primary source, request correction of a factual item record, or publish
an eligible Open Question. Their product-benefit statements remain Commercial
claim. They cannot publish a Finding about an affiliated product as an
independent athlete object in v1.

## Moderation and misinformation contract

### Required operations

Complete v1 includes:

- public-object eligibility and review queues;
- higher-risk reply queues;
- identity, credential, affiliation, and conflict review;
- report intake and triage;
- deterministic temporary hiding;
- thread lock;
- user mute, block, removal, and suspension;
- object correction, withdrawal, and unpublish;
- Pack publication pause and Pack discussion pause;
- moderator assignment;
- appeal;
- source correction and retraction state;
- append-only moderation audit events while legitimately retained; and
- published support contact information.

Users accept Community Rules before their first public submission. Separate
controls exist for Report object, Report reply, Report account, and Block
account. Report reasons include Safety issue, Unsupported or misleading claim,
Undisclosed commercial relationship, Copied dose or protocol, Harassment,
Impersonation or false credential, and Other.

Apple's current App Review Guidelines require user-generated content products to
filter objectionable material, support reporting and timely response, let users
block abusive users, and publish contact information
([App Review Guidelines 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content)).
Those are minimum platform conditions, not a complete safety strategy.

Google Play also requires robust ongoing UGC moderation, accepted terms,
in-app reporting of content and users, and blocking
([Google Play UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en)).
Its separate health-content policy makes a Health apps declaration, privacy
wording, harmful functionality, health misinformation, and supplement claims
material planning risks
([Google Play Health Content and Services](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511?hl=en)).
The safe planning assumption is that Heat and Performance Supplementation put
the product in scope until store preparation establishes otherwise.

If the service is within the EU Digital Services Act's scope, notice and action,
reasons for restrictions, complaints, and human supervision can become
additional product obligations. Exact classification and any small-platform
exception require counsel
([Regulation (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065)).

### Review model

Pre-publication human review is required for:

- Practice Guides and Pack versions;
- Practitioner Protocol Notes;
- all Heat and Performance Supplementation Findings;
- commercially conflicted public objects;
- newly checked commercial representatives' first contributions;
- objects or replies held by a disease-claim, copied-dose, undisclosed-conflict,
  or prohibited-substance rule;
- identity, credential, and affiliation states; and
- appealed safety decisions.

Race Fueling Findings, Research Notes, Open Questions, and low-risk replies may
publish after deterministic eligibility checks during the invitation cohort,
with post-publication review and reporting. A Pack can move an object class to
pre-publication review when risk or workload changes.

Automated checks may detect missing required fields, prohibited phrases,
unsupported claims, commercial disclosures, source metadata, duplicates, and
private-data patterns. They may temporarily hold or hide. They may not issue a
credential, approve health-adjacent content alone, adjudicate a scientific
dispute, or rewrite an author's meaning.

### Capacity gate

Community launch requires:

- one named primary and one named backup moderator;
- coverage hours displayed internally and supported by temporary-hide rules;
- a critical safety escalation path;
- a normal-report target of 24 hours during the invitation cohort;
- an appeal target with named ownership;
- a tested Pack-wide discussion pause;
- workload rehearsal with non-public policy scenarios; and
- evidence that the queue stays inside its operating cap for four consecutive
  weeks before cohort expansion.

These targets are product operations hypotheses, not legal guarantees. They
must be revised from observed workload.

### Misinformation controls

1. Claims receive explicit labels and required source fields.
2. Popularity never changes rank or evidence.
3. Commercial connections appear next to relevant content.
4. Research Notes show population, result, limitations, conflicts, and
   correction state.
5. Findings show the personal context and revision.
6. Source screenshots do not substitute for direct source links.
7. Corrected or retracted sources propagate a visible review state to dependent
   objects.
8. Safety reports can trigger temporary hiding without declaring the author
   malicious.
9. Moderators can require correction or reject publication but cannot silently
   edit an observation or interpretation.
10. Repeated universal, causal, dosing, medical, or undisclosed promotional
    claims can suspend publication privileges.

## Safety and prohibited behavior

The product does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medical clearance,
return-to-sport advice, illness or injury protocols, disordered-eating or
body-composition protocols, medication or hormone protocols, doping protocols,
dangerous heat targets, app-generated supplement doses, deficiency guidance,
guaranteed performance claims, or universal claims from personal Findings.

The chosen coach or practitioner stays authoritative for instruction inside the
existing plan. The app stops or limits the flow when the instruction crosses a
safety, publication, evidence, privacy, or data-integrity boundary.

Apple says apps that risk physical harm may be rejected and reviews inaccurate
medical information with greater scrutiny
([App Review Guidelines 1.4](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#physical-harm)).
This reinforces the non-medical positioning, but App Store compliance does not
prove clinical or legal safety.

The IOC supplement consensus notes that only a small set of supplements has
good evidence in specific scenarios, responses vary, expert assistance can be
important, and supplement use can create health, performance, or anti-doping
risks
([IOC consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29540367/)).

## Privacy, export, and deletion

### Data separation

- Experiments and raw Field Notes are private by default.
- A public object is a separate minimized revision.
- Exact routes, exact locations, private dates, source screenshots, raw sensor
  streams, and unpublished notes are excluded from public objects.
- Supplementation baseline is private.
- Moderators see the proposed public revision and eligibility attestations, not
  raw Field Notes, unless the athlete explicitly submits a specific excerpt.
- Credential evidence is private and access logged.

### Export

The athlete can export:

- profile and settings;
- role and verification history;
- Pack and Protocol versions referenced by their records;
- Experiments, Tries, Field Notes, comparisons, and Findings;
- saved objects, follows, and followed discussions;
- public objects and revisions;
- moderation decisions concerning their content; and
- deletion and unpublish receipts.

The export distinguishes private records from public revisions and includes
human-readable data plus a documented machine-readable form.

### Scoped deletion and unpublish

The athlete may delete a draft, Field Note, Experiment, saved object,
supplement baseline, public object, or account through explicit scoped actions.
Dependencies are explained before confirmation.

Unpublishing removes the substantive public object immediately. Direct links
show a content-free withdrawn tombstone when needed for correction or discussion
integrity. Descendant replies may remain only when they do not reveal the
withdrawn content or private information.

### Account deletion

Account deletion:

1. offers export first without making export mandatory;
2. immediately disables the public profile and unpublishes authored objects;
3. removes follow relationships and publication access;
4. enters a disclosed private-data deletion process;
5. removes credential evidence and sensitive documents according to the
   disclosed retention schedule;
6. retains only minimum legally or operationally required audit facts, without
   retaining public athlete narratives; and
7. provides a deletion receipt.

Apple requires in-app account deletion when an app supports account creation
([App Review Guidelines 5.1.1(v)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#data-collection-and-storage)).
Apple's deletion guidance explicitly includes shared user-generated content
([Offering account deletion in your app](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/)).
Google Play additionally requires a readily discoverable web deletion path and
deletion of associated user data
([Google Play User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)).

Complete v1 therefore includes both an authenticated in-app deletion path and a
public web request path for a user who cannot access the app. Deletion removes
or irreversibly severs private records, all authored public objects and replies,
relationships, subscriptions to discussions, cached or indexed copies under
the operator's control, and processor copies through their documented paths.

The desire for immutable corrections is not by itself a demonstrated retention
ground. A moderation audit retains only the minimum data supported by a
documented legal or legitimate ground, for a declared period, with access
controls and disclosure. Exact retention, backups, subscription handling, and
proof of deletion need launch-jurisdiction privacy and legal review.

## Accessibility, reliability, and premium behavior

Premium means trustworthy pace and finish quality.

- Experiment setup targets three minutes after onboarding.
- A routine Field Note targets under 90 seconds.
- All required states work with keyboard and assistive technology where the
  platform supports them.
- Text resizing, screen reader labels, contrast, touch targets, and focus order
  are release criteria.
- Color never carries evidence, authority, safety, or comparison meaning alone.
- Offline drafts show local, syncing, retrying, conflicted, and saved states.
- Retry is idempotent and never creates duplicate Field Notes or posts.
- Reduced motion preserves every state transition without animation.
- Notifications cover chosen reminders, followed-discussion replies, Pack
  safety or correction notices, and credential expiry. They do not use likes,
  streaks, scarcity, or social-pressure prompts.

The Split Ledger and Hold Line remain the preferred visual mechanism because
they express planned versus actual behavior without implying clinical
measurement or a universal score. This task does not authorize Figma work.

## Value propositions

### 1. Keep the plan, improve the practice

You already have a training plan, and you may already trust a coach or
practitioner. The Performance Practice System helps you rehearse a bounded
fueling, heat, or supplementation practice inside that plan, record what you
intended and what actually happened, and decide what is worth keeping,
repeating, changing, or stopping without silently rewriting your training.

### 2. A Finding you can inspect

Instead of a score or confident recommendation, every Finding keeps the Tries,
context, deviations, app comparisons, interpretation, and limitations that
produced it. You can revise the Finding when new practice changes your view, and
another athlete can learn from it without mistaking your experience for proof.

### 3. A performance community that preserves sources

Follow real people and Practice Packs in chronological feeds. Research,
practitioner practice, commercial claims, athlete observations, app
comparisons, and personal interpretations stay visibly distinct, while
credentials and popularity never change the evidence status of a statement.

### 4. Turn useful discussion into your own Practice

When a Finding, Research Note, Practitioner Protocol Note, or Open Question
matters to you, start a linked private Experiment that preserves attribution
but rebuilds the question, authority, eligibility, targets, and safety for your
own context. The social endpoint is a better private decision, not more feed
engagement.

## Success, simplification, and kill criteria

These are hypotheses to validate after implementation authority and ethical
recruitment exist. No athlete outreach occurs in this task.

### Private success

Continue when:

- an invited athlete completes a real Experiment and voluntarily begins a
  second;
- median first Experiment setup is at or below three minutes after onboarding;
- median routine Field Note completion is at or below 90 seconds;
- at least half of completed Findings change or confirm a Keep, Repeat, Change
  one thing, Stop, or Not enough evidence decision the athlete considers useful;
- Race Fueling and Heat feel like Packs in one system without sharing a false
  metric; and
- the product remains useful when Feed has no people or public Findings.

### Social success

Continue when:

- athletes can distinguish the nine evidence labels in comprehension testing;
- following a person or Pack improves discovery without changing perceived
  evidence strength;
- a meaningful share of high-intent public-object sessions ends in Save,
  Follow discussion, or Start linked Experiment rather than passive scrolling;
- linked Experiments never copy a target, dose, heat exposure, authority, or
  eligibility;
- structured replies produce source, context, or interpretation value instead
  of generic encouragement; and
- public counts can remain hidden without making profiles incomprehensible.

### Operations success

Continue when:

- moderation meets the cohort's stated targets without exposing raw Field
  Notes;
- fewer than one in five submitted public objects needs substantive meaning
  rewritten to become eligible;
- credential rechecks and appeals have named ownership;
- commercial disclosures appear next to every relevant object;
- retracted or corrected sources can propagate review state; and
- a Pack-wide publication or discussion pause works without breaking private
  Practice.

### Simplification criteria

Simplify the contract before expansion when:

- athletes repeatedly confuse Practice family, Pack, Protocol, and Experiment;
- one Pack needs generic fields that have no decision value;
- more than one intended decision variable becomes the default;
- discussion depth creates repeated context loss;
- commercial item pages require bespoke rules that do not generalize to Tested
  Items;
- optional onboarding questions delay the first Try; or
- moderation categories exceed the team's ability to apply them consistently.

Simplification means fewer optional fields, fewer Pack variants, clearer
eligibility, narrower launch jurisdiction, or a smaller invitation cohort. It
does not mean deleting accepted complete-v1 surfaces and calling the result v1.

### Kill criteria

Stop or change the private thesis when:

- Findings do not change or confirm a decision after a meaningful set of real
  Experiments;
- Field Notes remain slower or less useful than Notes;
- athletes routinely interpret App comparison as causation despite the
  interface;
- unsupported custom topics dominate use;
- Race Fueling, Heat, and Supplementation require unrelated core loops; or
- athletes prefer existing focused tools after two real Experiments.

Stop or change the social thesis when:

- public contribution becomes generic workout posting;
- role or popularity cues are persistently read as evidence;
- most discussions do not add context, a source, a related observation, or a
  bounded challenge;
- linked private Experiments are rare enough that Feed becomes an end in
  itself;
- commercial pressure repeatedly defeats disclosure and ranking boundaries;
- moderation requires substantive rewriting of most objects; or
- the team cannot provide safe coverage, corrections, appeals, and credential
  review.

A failed social thesis does not authorize quietly shipping a private-only
reduced v1 under this contract. It requires an explicit product decision.
