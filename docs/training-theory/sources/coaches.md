# Coach source notes

Coach material is used to document practice, generate testable hypotheses, and understand session organization. It is never treated as scientific confirmation by authority alone.

For each coach source, record the exact claim, population usually coached, whether research is cited, proposed mechanism, related scientific source IDs, limitations, costs, and road/trail/ultra applicability.

## First-release coverage

### SWAP / David and Megan Roche

Authenticated Patreon material was read through the user's logged-in browser session and synthesized without reproducing paid articles. The current coverage includes:

- semi-structured easy/moderate running;
- improving bike power as a runner;
- fatigue resistance/durability;
- the experimental “fatigue resistance snack.”
- the “Core Snack” adherence-oriented core routine;
- a personal hamstring isometric exercise;
- a bone-density article whose exercise claims were separated from clinical, supplement, hormone, and medication claims.

Public Trail Runner material adds the recurring use of strides/short hills, low-intensity control, caution around fasted training, minimal-dose resistance, and Mountain Legs. Strength-related coach sources are indexed as [SRC-055]–[SRC-060].

The repository separates:

- what SWAP visibly programs;
- the mechanism the coaches propose;
- what independent literature supports;
- what remains coach judgment or hypothesis;
- trail/ultra logic that does not transfer directly to road racing.
- general-population strength evidence that does not directly validate an endurance routine;
- performance, tissue-property, pain, and injury-incidence claims as separate outcomes.

### Marius Bakken / Norwegian Method

Bakken's original and updated articles are treated as primary historical/practice records, not controlled scientific evidence. They are indexed as [SRC-030] and [SRC-031]. The independent comparison layer uses threshold reviews, intensity-distribution synthesis, and the Norwegian best-practice study [SRC-006], [SRC-008], [SRC-028], and [SRC-029].

## Authentication and copyright boundary

- Store canonical URLs and access dates; never store credentials or session details.
- A paid source may be summarized and evaluated, but its full text should not be copied into the repository.
- Access status does not increase evidence weight.
- If a future agent cannot access a paid source, it should preserve the registered summary and mark current verification unavailable rather than inventing content.

## Adding another coaching system

Before creating a methodology document:

1. locate primary material from the coach or system;
2. reconstruct principles before collecting workouts;
3. identify the population and event context;
4. map each principle to independent evidence or mark it untested;
5. record likely stimulus, cost, best fit, poor fit, and failure modes;
6. compare the system with alternatives without turning the page into advocacy.
